Attack on Insaniyat and Kashmiriyat by Pakistan: PM Modi

Katra, Friday – Prime Minister Narendra Modi strongly condemned Pakistan, accusing it of attacking “Insaniyat” (humanity) and “Kashmiriyat” by targeting tourism in the Pahalgam Valley. He said the motive behind these attacks was to harm the livelihood of the Kashmiri people who depend on tourism for employment.

"Tourism brings jobs and prosperity to Kashmir, but Pakistani terrorists don’t want that," PM Modi said. He added that their goal is to destabilize the region and provoke communal tensions in India.

Modi Ji Inaugurates First-Ever Rail Service in Kashmir Valley

On June 6, Prime Minister Modi inaugurated the first-ever rail service in the Kashmir Valley. He launched the 272 km long USBRL project, which is considered one of the most challenging railway projects in India. It also includes the world’s highest railway bridge, built over the Chenab River.

Along with this, Prime Minister Modi also inaugurated the Vande Bharat train service between Katra and Srinagar,Which now has a travel time of 3 hours.

Highlighting the Indian armed forces' recent cross-border strikes using BrahMos missiles, Modi showcased India's defense capabilities under the 'Make in India' initiative. He also announced infrastructure and defense projects in Uttar Pradesh worth over ₹47,600 crore, aiming to boost self-reliance and industrial growth

Intra-Party Dissent Surges Across Telangana's Political Spectrum

Telangana is witnessing unprecedented intra-party dissent within major political parties—BRS, BJP, and Congress. India and Pakistan Near Completion of Border Troop Reduction

India and Pakistan are close to completing a troop drawdown along their border, restoring military levels to those before the April 2025 conflict. The recent escalation, triggered by a deadly attack in Indian Kashmir, led to intense fighting. Despite a ceasefire, Pakistani General Sahir Shamshad Mirza warned that broader targeting beyond Kashmir marks a dangerous trend, emphasizing the need for structured crisis management mechanisms and peaceful resolution through dialogue.

Nitish Kumar to Lead NDA in 2025 Bihar Assembly Polls

Bihar BJP president Dilip Jaiswal confirmed that the NDA will contest the 2025 assembly polls under Chief Minister Nitish Kumar's leadership. This announcement follows Kumar's recent political realignment with the BJP, abandoning the INDIA bloc he helped form. Despite speculations about his declining popularity, Kumar's maneuvering has secured the backing of the BJP and its allies, positioning him as a central figure in Bihar's political landscape.

West Bengal Teachers Protest Over Recruitment Cancellations

On May 15, over a thousand sacked teachers protested outside the West Bengal Education Department in Kolkata, demanding reinstatement without fresh recruitment exams. The demonstration escalated into clashes with police, resulting in injuries. In response, the state cabinet approved the West Bengal Livelihood and Social Security Interim Scheme, providing financial assistance to affected staffers until their employment status is resolved.

Operation Abhyaas: India's Largest Civil Defence Drill Conducted

On May 7, India executed 'Operation Abhyaas', its most extensive civil defence drill since 1971, across 244 districts. The exercise included air raid sirens, blackout simulations, and evacuation drills, aiming to bolster civilian preparedness amid escalating tensions with Pakistan. Major cities like Delhi, Mumbai, and Hyderabad participated, enhancing readiness against potential hostile threats.

Congress Initiates 'Jai Hind Sabha' to Counter BJP's Narrative

The Congress party launched the 'Jai Hind Sabha' in Jabalpur on May 31, aiming to challenge the BJP's ideological stance. State president Jitu Patwari emphasized the event as the beginning of a new ideological struggle, focusing on preserving democratic values and countering dictatorial tendencies. The move signifies Congress's efforts to rejuvenate its political strategy in Madhya Pradesh.

Haryana CM Praises Nationwide Caste Census Initiative

Haryana Chief Minister Nayab Singh Saini lauded Prime Minister Narendra Modi's decision to implement a nationwide caste census, describing it as a visionary step toward social justice. He emphasized that the move would empower marginalized communities and criticized the Congress for previously politicizing and delaying such initiatives.

Congress Plans 'Samvidhan Bachao Rashtriya Padayatra'

The Congress Working Committee announced a 13-month-long campaign, 'Samvidhan Bachao Rashtriya Padayatra', starting January 26, 2025. The initiative aims to uphold constitutional values and counter the BJP's ideological influence. The party also plans to overhaul its organization to prepare for future elections, marking a strategic shift in its political approach.

Chhattisgarh Local Elections: BJP Achieves Landslide Victory

In the 2025 Chhattisgarh local body elections held on February 11, the BJP secured a significant win, capturing 126 out of 173 municipalities. The Congress managed only 30 seats, marking a substantial decline. The results indicate a strong consolidation of BJP's influence in the state, reflecting shifting political dynamics.

Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025 Sparks Nationwide Protests

The enactment of the Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025 led to widespread protests across India, particularly in West Bengal and Tripura. Critics, including the All India Muslim Personal Law Board, argue that the law undermines Muslim religious endowments. The government maintains that the reforms aim to ensure transparency in waqf property management.

One Nation, One Election Bill Faces Opposition in Parliament

The proposed 'One Nation, One Election' bill, aiming to synchronize Lok Sabha and Assembly elections, has been referred to a Joint Committee of Parliament. The BJP faces challenges in securing the necessary majority, with significant opposition from various parties concerned about the implications for federalism and democratic processes.

Nagpur Witnesses Diverse Developments on May 30

Nagpur experienced a range of significant events on May 30, including medical breakthroughs like CAR T-cell therapy, infrastructure achievements such as the completion of the Nag River arch bridge, and social initiatives like Operation Shodh, which reunited 392 missing persons. The city also addressed challenges like rising COVID-19 cases and civic neglect.

Khelo India Youth Games 2025 Conclude Successfully in Bihar

The 7th edition of the Khelo India Youth Games concluded on May 15, 2025, in Bihar, marking the first time the event was held in the state. Over 10,000 athletes from 36 states and union territories competed across 28 disciplines. The event showcased India's commitment to promoting sports and nurturing young talent.
